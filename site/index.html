Trackers built to outlast the asset

Three device families cover everything from trailers to roll cages. The demo simulates how each one reports.

Smart tracker

GNSS, Wi-Fi and network positioning over LTE-M. Reports every 15 minutes in motion and every 6 hours at rest, with a multi-year battery.

BLE location tag

A low-cost tag for high-volume assets. Gateways inside geozones pick it up, so you get site-level presence at scale.

Condition sensor

Adds temperature and fill level for IBCs and sensitive goods, with NB-IoT connectivity and threshold alerts.
